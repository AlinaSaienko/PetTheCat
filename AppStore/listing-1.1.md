## Description (max 4000)
Need a quiet moment? Pet the cat.

Pocket Purr is a small, calming app with one simple idea: a cat on your screen that you can stroke with your finger. Slide your finger over the fur and it ruffles and settles back. Find the spot your cat likes and the purr starts soft, then grows the longer you keep petting. Let go, and it slowly fades away.

NEW: CHRISTMAS SURPRISE BOXES
Open a free surprise box and discover a festive cat: Gingerbread, Reindeer, Elf, Frost, Cocoa, and one secret cat that is very hard to find. A free box arrives every day from December 1 to January 6. Get the same cat three times and you receive a bonus box. All boxes are free, there is nothing to buy, and the odds are always shown before you open one.

EVERY SPOT IS DIFFERENT
• Chin and cheeks: pure bliss
• Forehead and back: happy purring
• Ears: pleasant, but not too much
• Belly: careful, it may be a trap
• Tail: just don't

A PURR YOU CAN HEAR AND FEEL
The purr is a real-time sound made right on your device, paired with soft vibration (on iPhones that support haptics), so it feels like a cat is purring in your hand. Turn the volume up and put it in your pocket if you like.

YOUR COLLECTION
Start with six classic cats: British Blue, Siamese, Ginger Tabby, Black, White and Grey Tabby. Add the Christmas cats you find in boxes and see them all in My cats.

A CAT ON YOUR HOME SCREEN
Add the widget, choose a cat, and tap it right on your Home Screen to make it purr, without even opening the app.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no internet connection needed. Pocket Purr collects nothing about you. If you use iCloud, your collection can sync between your own devices through your iCloud account.

Take a breath. Pet the cat.

## App Review Information — Notes
No login or account is required. The app has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads, and it uses no servers or third-party services.

NEW IN 1.1 — Christmas surprise boxes (all free):
1. The gift icon at the top right of the main screen shows the number of unopened boxes. Tap it to open a box (tap the box 3 times). The "Odds" button on the box screens shows the exact probability of every cat before opening.
2. Free boxes are given one per day from Dec 1, 2026 to Jan 6, 2027, plus one welcome box with the update. Outside the season, to get more boxes for testing, long-press the gift icon for 2 seconds: this adds one box.
3. The grid icon at the top left opens "My cats", the collection of found cats. Found Christmas cats can be selected and petted like the classic cats. Getting the same cat 3 times gives one bonus box.
4. The collection is stored on the device and in the user's own iCloud key-value storage so it can sync between their devices. We have no server and receive no data.

Basic use: drag a finger over the cat; sound and haptics play while petting (turn the volume up; haptics need a supported iPhone). Widget: long-press the Home Screen → + → Pocket Purr (iOS 17 or later). Dark mode follows the system setting.
