PetTheCat

A calm little app for petting a cat.

In short: PetTheCat does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

What the app does not collect

PetTheCat does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, device identifiers, usage data or any other personal information. It contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software.

What stays on your device

To work properly the app keeps a few small settings on your device only, for example which cat you picked and when the Home Screen widget cat was last petted. This information never leaves your device, is not sent to the developer or anyone else, and is deleted when you delete the app.

Sound and vibration

The app plays a purring sound and uses your phone's haptic motor (vibration) while you pet the cat. Both are generated on your device. The app does not use the microphone.

Home Screen widget

The optional widget shows your chosen cat and reacts when you tap it. It runs entirely on your device and does not collect or transmit any data.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it is safe for people of all ages.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
